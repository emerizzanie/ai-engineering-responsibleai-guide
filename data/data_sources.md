## Notes

- S01 to S04 are the main sources. S05 is optional.
- Only S01 is prepared into text chunks so far. See the [Week 3 data inventory](../docs/week3_data_inventory.md).
- **Rights:** I checked the rights statement inside each PDF in Week 4. NIST is free of charge and UNESCO is Open Access (CC-BY-SA 3.0 IGO). The Malaysia AIGE and ASEAN PDFs say they may not be copied without permission, so keeping them in this repo is an open issue. See [section E of the Week 4 audit](../docs/week4_data_quality_audit.md#e-responsible-data-practices).
